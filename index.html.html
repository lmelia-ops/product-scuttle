How to play: Each round, dice are rolled and all players see the same digits. Arrange them into two numbers and multiply on paper. After 3 rounds, add your products. The player with the lowest total above the minimum sum wins. If nobody clears the minimum, the highest total wins. 2-player tip: Player 2 reads their numbers and answer aloud, and Player 1 types it in.
